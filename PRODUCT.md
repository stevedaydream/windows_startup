# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
Native Windows desktop (WPF).
## Users
Windows users identifying and enabling or disabling startup entries.
## Product Purpose
Make startup entries clear and straightforward to manage.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve .NET 8 WPF, MVVM and existing functionality: registry and Startup folder entries, reversible enable/disable, add, remove, source and executable locations, administrator restart. Services and scheduled tasks are out of scope.
## Confirmed Direction
The user requested an overall redesign emphasizing clear information and intuitive operation.
